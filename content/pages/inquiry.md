---
title: Inquiry
lede: An inquiry begins with context, not commitment.
---

Arcbrim Builders engages selectively. The purpose of an inquiry is to determine alignment before any scope, pricing, or scheduling discussion.

## What to include

An effective inquiry provides enough context to evaluate fit. Helpful details include:

- Project type and location  
- Desired timeline  
- Budget range (approximate is fine)  
- Decision-maker involvement  
- Any constraints or considerations already known  

Incomplete inquiries may delay response.

## What happens next

If alignment appears likely, we’ll respond to continue the conversation. This may include a brief call or a request for additional detail.

If the project is not a fit, we’ll say so directly. That clarity is intentional.
